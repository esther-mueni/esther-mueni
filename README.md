# Hi, I am Esther Mueni 👋

Product Manager · Vibe Coder · Writer

I love building solutions and telling stories. I started as an embedded systems engineer working on IoT gadgets, transitioned into product management, and now work as a product manager with strong vibe coding skills — using them to exercise my problem-solving muscle and build real systems.

Right now, I am building systems for **Attic Chapter**, a youth entrepreneurship community, and occasionally ship hobby projects to flex my vibe coding skills. When my credits run out, I write — on LinkedIn or Substack — about product, tech, and building in African markets.

## 🚀 Things I have Built
 
 
### 1. Attic Chapter Member Portal
> *From scattered spreadsheets to a community that runs itself.*
 
A youth entrepreneurship community was tracking members, events and payments by hand. I built the system that replaced it: members sign up, pay, RSVP and renew on their own, and organisers see everything in one place. Renewals, grace periods and payment-gated RSVPs all just work.

#### 2. NeedThis
> *Let people ask for your time without ever sharing your email.*
 
One link is all you share. Anyone can send you a request, you approve or decline with a click, and approved requests land straight on your calendar. No back-and-forth, no inbox clutter.

#### 4. Smart Knock Sensor
> *A knock you can't hear becomes a text you can't miss.*
 
Before the web apps, I built hardware. This is a door-knock detector I designed end to end, from the sensor and circuit to the firmware, that sends an SMS the moment someone knocks.
